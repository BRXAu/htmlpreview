BHP Xplor Showcase: An Investor Event
 
 
17 September 2026
09:00-18:00
Well& by Durst, 151 West 42nd Street, New York
 
BHP Xplor and 121 Mining Investment are hosting an invitation-only event connecting active mining investors with the 2026 BHP Xplor cohort, ten exploration and technology companies advancing projects with real growth potential.

This is a curated deal-sourcing opportunity. Every company has been selected to participate in BHP Xplor through a rigorous technical review process. The event gives investors early visibility to where they stand now.
 
 
Why investors attend
 
Direct access to CEOs and technical leaders, ahead of wider market awareness.
A pre-vetted pipeline, filtered for geological merit and growth potential by BHP's technical team.
An intimate, deal-focused setting where investors can speak directly with each company.
 
 
Attendance is limited to active investors and subject to approval.
