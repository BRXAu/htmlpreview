Campaign Manager 360 (DCM) validator

BRX's usual check, at h5validator.appspot.com/dcm. All three returned the same result. The two flags are expected: CM360 and Google Ads App campaigns handle clicks differently.

Why it flags

Missing click tag (error). CM360 sets the click-through through a clickTag variable. Google Ads App campaigns require ExitApi.exit() instead, and reject custom click tags, so these banners don't have one.

4th party call (warning). The banners load Google's own exitapi.js, which Google Ads App campaigns require.

If any placement is trafficked through CM360, it needs a CM360 version of each banner: the same build with a clickTag exit and without exitapi.js. That's a small build change.
